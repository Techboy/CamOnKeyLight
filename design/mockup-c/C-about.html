About Cam On Key Light

Cam On Key Light

Version 0.9.0

Turns your Elgato Key Lights on and off when your webcam turns on or off. You can also hold the lights on or off yourself.

Cam On Key Light is an independent app. It is not made, sold or endorsed by Elgato.
